Frequently asked

Questions merchants ask before applying

Is a merchant cash advance a loan?

No. A merchant cash advance is a purchase and sale of future receivables. ACA Funding Group pays a purchase price today for a specified dollar amount of receivables your business will generate. Because it is a purchase rather than a loan, there is no interest rate, no annual percentage rate, and no fixed maturity date.

The total specified amount, the percentage of receivables purchased, and the remittance frequency are all disclosed in the receivables purchase agreement before signing.

What documents do you need to review a file?

A completed application, the last 3–4 months of complete business bank statements, a government-issued photo ID for the signing owner, and a voided business check. Depending on the industry we may also request merchant processing statements, a copy of the entity formation documents, or a current lease.

Do you check personal credit?

Credit is reviewed as one input among several, but our underwriting is driven primarily by the bank statements: deposit volume and consistency, average daily balance, negative days, and existing remittance obligations. Businesses declined by banks on credit alone are regularly approved here.

How fast can funding happen?

Complete files are typically reviewed within 24 hours. Once a term sheet is accepted and bank verification is complete, funding is generally sent the same or next business day.

I already have one or more advances. Can I still be reviewed?

Yes. Existing positions are common and do not automatically disqualify a business. We look at the aggregate daily or weekly obligation relative to collections. In some cases the right answer is a consolidation that pays off multiple positions and leaves a single remittance schedule.

How does remittance actually work?

Remittance is collected by ACH on a daily (business-day) or weekly schedule, set against a specified percentage of the receivables purchased. Where a fixed estimated amount is debited, reconciliation is available so the amount collected tracks actual receivables.

Do you work with ISOs and brokers?

Yes. We accept submissions from independent sales organizations and brokers and provide direct underwriter contact on live files. Reach out using the contact details on the About page to discuss a partner agreement.

Is funding available in every state?

No. Availability depends on the business location, entity type, and industry, and offerings are not available in all states. Eligibility is confirmed during underwriting.
