What you'll need

  • Last 3–4 months of complete business bank statements
  • Government-issued photo ID for the signing owner
  • Voided business check for the operating account

  • Payoff letters, if consolidating existing positions

What happens next

  • Underwriting review, typically within 24 hours
  • Written term sheet if the file supports a purchase
  • Signature and bank verification
  • Funding the same or next business day

Remember

This is an application to sell a specified amount of future receivables — not a loan application. No interest rate or APR applies, and no offer exists until a receivables purchase agreement is executed.
