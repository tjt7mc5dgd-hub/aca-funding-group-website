A direct funder with underwriting in the building

ACA Funding Group LLC is a New Jersey based funding company that purchases future receivables from small and mid-sized businesses using its own capital.

Who we are

Our own capital, our own decisions

ACA Funding Group LLC operates as a direct funder. When we issue a term sheet, we are the party purchasing the receivables and deploying the capital — not a broker packaging your file for someone else to price. That difference shows up in the way files move: fewer hand-offs, direct answers on conditions, and a person who can explain exactly why a structure looks the way it does.

Underwriting is handled in house. Files are reviewed against business bank statements — deposit consistency, average daily balance, negative days, and existing remittance obligations — rather than against a single credit score. That lets us work through the kinds of files automated systems reject outright: uneven seasons, a soft month, prior positions, or an owner whose personal credit does not reflect how the business actually performs.

We also fund through partners. Independent sales organizations and brokers submit files to us directly and work with the same underwriters who make the decision, with clear submission requirements and straight answers on what a file needs.

How we operate

Principles we hold to on every file

Structure disclosed first
Purchase price, specified amount, specified percentage, and remittance frequency are stated in writing before signature.
No cost to be reviewed
Applications and statement reviews carry no charge and no obligation to accept a term sheet.
Right-sized, not maxed out
We size the purchase to what collections support, because a remittance the business cannot carry helps no one.
